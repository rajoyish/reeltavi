# ReelTavi Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

ReelTavi is a Chrome extension that adds playback controls and downloads to Facebook videos. It's built to work entirely on your computer.

## What ReelTavi does not do

- It doesn't collect, store or send personal information, browsing history, or anything about the pages you visit to the developer or anyone else.
- It has no servers, analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- It doesn't read your messages, posts, friends list or account details.

## Data handled on your device

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Your settings (show control bar, double-click fullscreen, auto-fullscreen, keyboard shortcuts) | To remember your choices | Chrome sync storage, synced by Chrome to your own Google account if you've turned on Chrome Sync |
| Your volume level and mute choice | To keep the same sound level from one Reel or video to the next | Chrome local storage on this computer |
| Video addresses found in the Facebook page you're viewing | To download the video you asked for | In memory, and in Chrome session storage while a download runs. Deleted when the download ends or the browser closes. |
| The video and audio of a video you choose to download | To build the MP4 file | Downloaded from Facebook's servers (`facebook.com`, `fbcdn.net`, `fbsbx.com`), processed in memory on your computer, and saved to your Chrome download folder |

ReelTavi only contacts Facebook's own servers, and only when you click **Download**.

## Permissions

- **Access to facebook.com, fbcdn.net and fbsbx.com:** to show the control bar on Facebook videos and fetch the video you choose to download.
- **downloads:** to save the finished file to your download folder and report its progress.
- **offscreen:** to build MP4 files in a hidden extension page so the Facebook tab stays responsive.
- **storage:** to save your settings and volume level, and track downloads in progress.
